Who We Are

Outdoor Fitness
Reimagined

Red Sea Strength is an Urban Outdoor Strength & Performance Hub based in Jeddah. We combine structured training, real results, and community — taken out of the gym and into the open.

01

Outdoor-First Approach

Train in parks, beaches, and private coastal resorts — an environment that motivates and energizes you every session.

02

Structured Performance Programs

Every session has purpose. Strength, conditioning, mobility — delivered by experienced coaches with real plans.

03

Community-Driven

We build more than fitness. We build belonging — a team that trains together, competes together, and grows together.

Vision
To become the leading outdoor fitness and performance community in Jeddah and expand across Saudi Arabia.
Mission
To provide accessible, structured, and high-quality outdoor training programs that improve physical health, build strong communities, and promote long-term fitness participation.
Industry
Sport · Recreation · Fitness · Community
